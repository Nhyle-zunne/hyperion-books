# Hyperion Books

**Hyperion Books** is an installable, offline-capable bookkeeping web app for recording business sales, expenses, payouts, and accounting transactions. It is designed for Philippine businesses and keeps records on your device.

**Open the app:** https://nhyle-zunne.github.io/hyperion-books/

## Features

- **Dashboard:** Revenue, Net Profit, Expenses, and Sales summaries; revenue and expense trends; sales breakdowns; recent transactions; and cash flow.
- **Sales:** Add sales received through QR Ph, bank transfer, cash, or other payment methods. Import sales CSV/Excel files, including supported PayMongo transaction exports. Track customer names and contact details where available.
- **Expenses and payouts:** Record business expenses and bank payouts. Import expense CSV files and review transactions.
- **Books of Accounts:** View and export the Cash Receipts Journal, Cash Disbursements Journal, General Journal, and General Ledger. Filter records by period and print or export reports.
- **Business records:** Manage customers, vendors, products, expense categories, purchases, assets, and payroll reference records.
- **Reports:** View financial summaries, management reports, and aging information based on recorded data.
- **Tax filing workspace:** Use a calendar, filing trackers, withholding-certificate records, and an invoice preparation register.
- **Search and exports:** Find local records, export Excel workbooks, and create or restore JSON backups.
- **Installation and appearance:** Add Hyperion Books to Android, iPhone/iPad, Windows, Mac, or Linux from a supported browser. Light and dark themes are available.

## Saving your records

Hyperion Books stores financial records **locally in your browser on your device**. There is no automatic account-based or cross-device synchronization.

- Use **Backups & Exports** to save a JSON backup regularly.
- Restoring a JSON backup **replaces existing data** on the destination device.
- Excel exports are reports, not restorable backups.
- Keep backups private. Exported backups are not encrypted.
- Clearing browser/site data or switching devices can make local records unavailable without a backup.

## Accounting and tax use

Books, reports, tax estimates, and invoice records are **working documents** based on the information entered or imported. Hyperion Books does not automatically file tax returns, issue BIR-authorized invoices, or register computerized Books of Accounts. Review the figures against original receipts, statements, and applicable BIR requirements before official use.
