# Basic accounting example

If PayMongo deposits ₱970 from ₱1,000 gross collections, with an *unclassified* ₱30 deduction, the provisional journal debits Cash/bank ₱970 and Platform deductions ₱30, and credits Provisional receipts ₱1,000. An expense of ₱50 debits its expense category and credits Cash/bank ₱50. The journal balances, and cash movement is ₱920.

This does not determine whether the ₱30 represents fees, refunds, tax withholding, or other adjustments. It also does not assert that settlement dates are dates of taxable sales.
